ClickIt Support

Find the shot. Own the spot. Answers to common questions about the ClickIt app.

Getting started

How do I create an account? Open ClickIt and tap Sign up. Enter your email and a password, then tap the confirmation link we email you. A short setup asks you to pick a username and a few interests, then you land on the map.
How do I find photo spots? The Map tab shows every spot as a pin; zoomed out, nearby pins group into numbered circles. Use the filters at the top (Nature, Urban, Golden Hour and more) and tap a pin to see photos, difficulty and the best time to shoot. The Explore tab lists spots with golden hour coming up and the most-visited spots.
How do I pin a new spot? Tap the Upload tab or the + button on the map. Take a photo or choose one from your library. If the photo has location data, ClickIt places the pin for you; if not, drop the pin yourself. Add a name, tags and difficulty, then publish. Your spot appears on the map right away, and you earn XP as its Founder.
What if someone already pinned that spot? If an existing spot is within 50 m of your photo, ClickIt offers to add your photo to it instead of creating a duplicate.

Features

What is golden hour? The soft, warm light just after sunrise and before sunset. ClickIt calculates it for every spot and shows a live countdown on the map. Turn on golden-hour alerts in Profile → Settings to get a heads-up 45 minutes before golden hour at spots you’ve saved within 20 km.
How do XP and ranks work? You earn XP for founding spots, adding photos, getting upvotes, completing challenges and winning Shot Duels. As XP grows you rank up from Bronze to Silver, Gold, Elite and Legend.
How do challenges work? Open the Challenges tab and tap Join. Each challenge shows what counts and your progress. Only actions after you join count.
What is a Shot Duel? If you and another photographer both have photos of the same spot, you can challenge them from the spot’s page. Once they accept, everyone else votes for 72 hours and the winner earns XP.
What are Photo walk and AR Compass? Both are on the Explore tab. Photo walk plans a 1–3 hour walking route through nearby spots, timed to reach the last one at golden hour. AR Compass shows which direction nearby spots are, on a radar or over your camera view.

Account

I forgot my password On the log in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and enter your email. We’ll send a link to set a new password.
How do I change my username or profile photo? Go to Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Edit profile.
How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away. If you can’t sign in, email us from your account’s email address and we’ll delete it for you.

Photos & safety

How do I remove a photo or a spot I posted? Email us with the spot name and we’ll remove it. Deleting your account also removes all your photos.
How do I report something inappropriate? Email us with the spot name or a screenshot. We review every report.
Is my location shared? Spots you publish are public, including their location. Your own location is never shown to other people. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Stay safe Never trespass or put yourself in danger for a shot. Check the difficulty rating and the weather before you go.
