Get help with the ClickIt app

Getting Started

How do I create an account? Open ClickIt and tap Sign Up. Enter your email and choose a password. You'll be taken straight to the map.
How do I find photo spots near me? The map on the home screen shows pins for all nearby photo spots. Tap any pin to see details, tips, and the best time to shoot.
How do I add a photo spot? Tap the + button on the map, drop a pin on the location, fill in the details, and submit. Your spot will appear on the map after approval.

Account

I forgot my password On the sign in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.
How do I change my username or profile photo? Go to your profile tab and tap Edit Profile. You can update your name, username, and profile photo from there.
How do I delete my account? Email us at support@clickitapp.com and we'll permanently delete your account and all associated data within 48 hours.

Posts & Community

How do I post a photo? Tap the camera icon in the Community tab, choose or take a photo, add a caption, and hit Post.
Can I delete my posts? Yes — tap the three dots on any of your posts and select Delete.
How do I report inappropriate content? Tap the three dots on any post or spot and select Report. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours.
