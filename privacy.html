Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, the spots and photos you post, and your activity in the app.
  • Spots and photos you post are public: anyone can see them, even without an account. Your profile is visible to other ClickIt users.
  • ClickIt does not store your location history. We store the location of spots you choose to publish, and send your approximate location to our map and weather providers so those features work.
  • No ads, no tracking for advertising, and we never sell your data.
  • Email clickit.app01@gmail.com to see or delete your data.

ClickIt is a photo-spot discovery app built as a student project. This policy explains what information the ClickIt app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our login provider; we never see your password), username, and the interests you pick during sign-up.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo and bio.
  • Spots and photos: the photos you upload, plus the spot name, description, tags, difficulty and gear you enter.

Information collected when you use ClickIt

  • Spot location: when you publish a photo, your phone reads the GPS position saved in the photo (or the pin you drop), and we store it with the spot, along with its city and country. Spot locations are public. The photo file itself is re-saved before upload, which removes all of its hidden metadata (including GPS and camera serial numbers).
  • Your current location (only if you allow it): used on your phone to center the map, show distances, plan photo walks, point the AR Compass and time golden-hour alerts. To show local weather, your location rounded to about 1 km is sent to OpenWeather. ClickIt does not store your current location or your location history.
  • Technical data: like most apps, the services ClickIt uses automatically log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, device type and time.
  • Activity: spots you save, spots you navigate to, upvotes, people you follow, challenges you join, Shot Duels and votes, and the XP, badges and rank you earn.
  • Camera: used to take photos for new spots and for the AR Compass live view. The AR Compass view is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Notifications: if you turn on golden-hour alerts, they are scheduled on your device. Your on/off choice is saved on your device.

What other users can see

  • Anyone, including people without an account: spots (name, description, location, tags, difficulty) and the photos posted to them, with the gear you entered and which account posted them.
  • Other ClickIt users: your username, profile photo, bio, rank, XP, badges, who you follow and who follows you, which photos you upvote, the spots you founded, and Shot Duels you take part in. Your rank can appear on leaderboards.
  • Only you: your email address, the spots you save, the spots you navigate to, the challenges you join and your progress, and how you voted in Shot Duels.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show spots on the map, display photos, keep you signed in, and calculate golden-hour times and walking routes.
  • To run the game parts: award XP and badges, track challenge progress, and run Shot Duels.
  • To send account emails, like confirming your email or resetting your password.
  • To keep ClickIt safe and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Map data collected by Mapbox

ClickIt's map is provided by Mapbox. The Mapbox map software collects de-identified device location data ("telemetry") to improve its maps, and it does not collect advertising identifiers. You can turn this off at any time: tap the ⓘ button at the bottom of the map, then choose the telemetry setting. Learn more at mapbox.com/telemetry. This is the only information a company collects through ClickIt for its own purposes. No one uses ClickIt to track you across other apps or websites.

Do Not Track

ClickIt does not track you across other apps or websites, and it treats every user the same whether or not a "Do Not Track" signal is sent.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and content while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, photos and activity. Spots you founded may stay on the map without your name, so other people's photos of them keep working.

Your choices

  • Edit your username, photo and bio any time in Profile → Settings → Edit profile.
  • Turn location, camera and notification access on or off in your phone's Settings. ClickIt still works with these off, with fewer features.
  • Turn golden-hour alerts off in Profile → Settings.
  • Turn off Mapbox map telemetry with the ⓘ button on the map.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and data, email clickit.app01@gmail.com from your account's email address.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your login session is stored in your phone's secure storage, and database rules only let you change your own information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

ClickIt is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
